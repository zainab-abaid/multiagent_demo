SECTION 1: COMPANY OVERVIEW AND HISTORY
================================================================================

Company Name: Chinook Music Store, Inc.
Legal Structure: Delaware C-Corporation
Headquarters: 1847 Market Street, Suite 400, San Francisco, CA 94103, USA
Founded: March 15, 2009
Founders: Marcus Chen (CEO), Sarah Williams (CTO), David Rodriguez (COO)

Mission Statement:
"To democratize access to music by providing a comprehensive, affordable, and user-friendly digital music marketplace that connects artists with fans worldwide."

Company Vision:
Chinook Music Store aspires to be the world's most trusted digital music retailer, known for exceptional catalog breadth, fair artist compensation, superior audio quality, and outstanding customer service.

Core Values:
1. Artist Respect: Fair compensation and transparent reporting for all artists
2. Customer Focus: Exceptional service and user experience
3. Musical Diversity: Comprehensive catalog across all genres and eras
4. Technical Excellence: Industry-leading audio quality and platform reliability
5. Global Accessibility: Music available to customers worldwide
6. Ethical Business: Transparent practices and sustainable operations

SECTION 3: PRODUCTS AND SERVICES
================================================================================

Core Products:

1. Individual Track Downloads
Description: Single track purchases in various formats
Formats Available: MP3 (256kbps, 320kbps), AAC, FLAC, ALAC
Pricing: $0.99 - $1.99 USD depending on track and quality
Delivery: Instant digital download
DRM: DRM-free files

2. Album Downloads
Description: Complete album purchases with discount
Formats: Same as individual tracks
Pricing: 10-15% discount from individual track prices
Bonus: Often includes digital booklet and artwork
Delivery: Instant download, zip file option available

3. Chinook Premium (Subscription)
Description: Monthly subscription for enhanced features
Price: $9.99/month or $99.99/year
Features:
- Early access to new releases
- Exclusive content and remixes
- Higher quality streaming previews
- Priority customer support
- Ad-free experience
- Monthly credit for free tracks

4. Chinook for Business
Description: B2B licensing for commercial use
Use Cases: Retail stores, restaurants, offices, gyms
Pricing: Custom based on venue size and usage
Features:
- Licensed for public performance
- Curated playlists by mood/genre
- Scheduling and automation tools
- Usage reporting and compliance

5. Chinook for Artists
Description: Artist services platform
Features:
- Direct upload and distribution
- Sales analytics and reporting
- Royalty tracking and payments
- Promotional tools
- Fan engagement features
Pricing: 15% commission on sales (vs 30% industry standard)

6. Gift Cards
Description: Prepaid credit for Chinook purchases
Denominations: $10, $25, $50, $100 USD
Availability: Digital delivery or physical cards
Redemption: Account credit, never expires

Audio Quality Tiers:

Standard Quality
- Format: MP3 256kbps
- File size: ~2.5MB per minute
- Price: Base price
- Best for: Casual listening, mobile with limited storage

High Quality
- Format: MP3 320kbps or AAC 256kbps
- File size: ~3MB per minute
- Price: Base price (included)
- Best for: Most listeners, good balance of quality and size

Lossless Quality
- Format: FLAC or ALAC
- File size: ~10MB per minute
- Price: +$0.50 per track
- Best for: Audiophiles, high-end audio systems

Masters Quality
- Format: High-resolution FLAC (24-bit/96kHz or higher)
- File size: ~25MB per minute
- Price: +$1.00 per track
- Availability: Select catalog only
- Best for: Professional use, ultimate quality

Platform Architecture:

Cloud Provider: Amazon Web Services (AWS)
Primary Region: US-West-2 (Oregon)
Secondary Regions: EU-West-1 (Ireland), AP-Northeast-1 (Tokyo)

Core Services:

- API: RESTful API with GraphQL options
- Database: PostgreSQL (primary), Redis (caching)
- Search: Elasticsearch
- CDN: CloudFront for global content delivery
- Storage: S3 for audio files and assets

Performance Metrics:
- Average page load time: 1.2 seconds
- API response time: 85ms average
- Uptime SLA: 99.95%

Security Measures:
- SSL/TLS encryption for all connections
- PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance for payments
- SOC 2 Type II certified
- Regular third-party security audits
- Bug bounty program active
- Two-factor authentication available
- Encrypted data at rest

Audio Processing Pipeline:

4. Quality Check: Automated and manual review
5. Metadata: Tag verification and enrichment
6. Storage: Redundant storage across regions
7. CDN: Distribution to edge locations
