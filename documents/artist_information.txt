Chinook Music Store - Artist Information

Body Count:
- Heavy metal band formed in 1990
- Known for blending metal with hip-hop influences
- Albums typically feature 15-20 tracks

Aerosmith:
- American rock band formed in 1970
- One of the best-selling rock bands of all time
- Known for hits like "Dream On" and "Walk This Way"
- Albums typically contain 10-15 tracks
- Strong following among classic rock enthusiasts

Led Zeppelin:
- English rock band formed in 1968

- Known for epic tracks and album-oriented releases
- Albums often feature longer tracks, with total album lengths exceeding 10 hours
- Highly sought after by collectors and rock music aficionados

Black Sabbath:
- English heavy metal band formed in 1968
- Pioneers of the heavy metal genre
- Albums typically feature 8-12 tracks
- Strong presence in the metal and hard rock sections

BackBeat:
- Rock band known for their energetic performances
- Albums typically feature 10-15 tracks

Black Label Society:
- American heavy metal band

- Albums typically feature 10-15 tracks

Caetano Veloso:

Marcos Valle:

- Brazilian composer, pianist, and songwriter

Gilberto Gil:
- Brazilian singer, guitarist, and songwriter

- Albums typically feature 10-15 tracks

Milton Nascimento & Bebeto:
