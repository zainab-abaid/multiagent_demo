Chinook Music Store - Pricing and Currency Policy

Pricing Structure:
- Individual tracks: $0.99 - $1.99 USD
- Average price per track: $1.05 USD (based on store-wide pricing data)
- Full albums: Typically 10-15% discount from sum of individual tracks
- Special promotions: Seasonal discounts up to 25% off

Currency Conversion:
The store accepts payments in multiple currencies. All transactions are converted to USD for accounting purposes.

Current Exchange Rates (approximate, for reference):
- 1 EUR = 0.85 USD (or 1 USD = 1.18 EUR)
- 1 GBP = 0.73 USD (or 1 USD = 1.37 GBP)
- 1 JPY = 0.009 USD (or 1 USD = 110 JPY)
- 1 CAD = 0.80 USD (or 1 USD = 1.25 CAD)
- 1 AUD = 0.74 USD (or 1 USD = 1.35 AUD)

Note: These rates are approximate and may vary. For accurate conversions, please use the current market rates or contact customer service.

Payment Processing:
All invoices are generated in USD. If a customer pays in a different currency, the amount is converted using the exchange rate at the time of purchase.
