SECTION 1: WELCOME AND COMPANY CULTURE
================================================================================

Welcome Message from the CEO:

Dear Team Member,

Welcome to Chinook Music Store! Whether you're joining us as a software engineer, customer support specialist, marketing professional, or any other role, you're now part of a team that's passionate about connecting people with the music they love.

Since founding Chinook in 2009, we've grown from a small startup to a global digital music retailer, but our core values remain the same: respect for artists, dedication to customers, and a commitment to building a workplace where everyone can thrive.

This handbook outlines our policies and expectations, but more importantly, it reflects who we are as a company. We believe in transparency, fairness, and treating every team member with respect.

I'm excited to have you on board. Together, we'll continue making Chinook the best place to discover and purchase music.

Welcome to the team!

Marcus Chen
CEO and Co-Founder

Company Values:

1. Music First
We're in business because we love music. Every decision we make should ultimately serve the goal of connecting people with the music that moves them.

2. Artist Respect
Artists create the products we sell. We treat them as partners, not just suppliers, and ensure they're fairly compensated for their work.

3. Customer Obsession
Our customers trust us with their music library. We earn that trust by providing excellent service, fair pricing, and a seamless experience.

4. Innovation
The music industry is constantly evolving. We embrace change, experiment boldly, and aren't afraid to try new approaches.

5. Integrity
We do the right thing, even when it's hard. We're honest with our customers, artists, partners, and each other.

6. Collaboration
Great music often comes from collaboration, and so does great work. We support each other, share knowledge, and celebrate collective success.

7. Diversity
Music is universal, and our team should reflect that. We value diverse perspectives and create an inclusive environment for all.

SECTION 2: EMPLOYMENT POLICIES
================================================================================

Employment Classification:

Full-Time Employees:
- Work 40 hours per week (standard)
- Eligible for full benefits package
- Receive salary and bonus eligibility
- Subject to exempt or non-exempt classification

Part-Time Employees:
- Work less than 30 hours per week
- Pro-rated benefits based on hours
- Hourly compensation
- Non-exempt classification

Temporary Employees:
- Fixed-term assignments
- Limited benefits
- May be converted to permanent

Contractors/Consultants:
- Not employees; independent contractor agreement
- No employee benefits
- Responsible for own taxes

Interns:
- Educational program participants
- Paid internships (minimum wage or above)
- Limited term (typically 3-6 months)
- Mentorship and learning focus

At-Will Employment:
Employment at Chinook is "at-will," meaning either the employee or the company may terminate employment at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice. This handbook does not constitute an employment contract.

Equal Employment Opportunity:
Chinook is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on:
- Race or color
- Religion or creed
- National origin or ancestry
- Sex, gender, or gender identity
- Sexual orientation
- Age
- Disability (physical or mental)
- Veteran status
- Genetic information
- Marital status
- Pregnancy or parental status
- Any other protected characteristic

Reasonable Accommodations:
Chinook provides reasonable accommodations for:
- Disabilities (ADA compliance)
- Religious practices
- Pregnancy and nursing
- Other protected needs

To request an accommodation, contact HR at hr@chinookmusic.com.

Background Checks:
All offers of employment are contingent upon:
- Verification of right to work
- Reference checks
- Background check (criminal history, where permitted)
- Education/credential verification (where relevant)

Background checks are conducted fairly and in compliance with all applicable laws, including "ban the box" regulations.

SECTION 3: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
================================================================================

Compensation Philosophy:
Chinook offers competitive compensation designed to attract and retain top talent. We benchmark salaries against market data annually and aim to pay at or above the 50th percentile for our industry and location.

Salary Administration:

Pay Periods:
- Salaried employees: Semi-monthly (15th and last day of month)
- Hourly employees: Bi-weekly (every other Friday)

Direct Deposit:
- Required for all employees
- Setup during onboarding
- Changes processed within 2 pay periods

Pay Transparency:
- Salary ranges published for all positions
- Level and compensation band communicated at hire
- Annual merit increases based on performance and market

Salary Ranges by Level (2024, San Francisco):

Individual Contributors:
- Level 1 (Entry): $60,000 - $80,000
- Level 2 (Junior): $75,000 - $100,000
- Level 3 (Mid): $95,000 - $130,000
- Level 4 (Senior): $125,000 - $170,000
- Level 5 (Staff): $160,000 - $210,000
- Level 6 (Principal): $200,000 - $280,000

Management:
- Manager: $120,000 - $170,000
- Senior Manager: $150,000 - $200,000
- Director: $180,000 - $250,000
- Senior Director: $220,000 - $300,000
- VP: $280,000 - $400,000

Note: Ranges adjusted for other locations based on cost of living.

Bonus Programs:

Annual Performance Bonus:
- Eligibility: All full-time employees after 6 months
- Target: 5-20% of base salary (based on level)
- Based on: Individual performance (50%) + Company performance (50%)
- Timing: Paid in March for prior year

Spot Bonuses:
- For exceptional contributions
- Manager nomination, VP approval
- Range: $500 - $5,000
- Can be awarded anytime

Referral Bonus:
- For successful employee referrals
- Amount: $2,500 - $10,000 (based on role level)
- Paid after referral completes 90 days

Benefits Overview:

Health Insurance:
- Medical: Choice of PPO or HMO plans
- Dental: PPO plan with orthodontia coverage
- Vision: Annual exams and frames/contacts allowance
- Coverage: Employee, spouse/partner, dependents
- Company contribution: 90% employee, 70% dependents

Retirement:
- 401(k) plan available immediately
- Company match: 100% up to 4% of salary
- Immediate vesting of company match
- Roth option available

Life and Disability:
- Basic life insurance: 2x annual salary (company paid)
- Supplemental life: Up to 5x salary (employee paid)
- Short-term disability: 60% of salary, up to 12 weeks
- Long-term disability: 60% of salary, after 12 weeks

Time Off:
- Vacation: See PTO policy below
- Sick leave: Unlimited (reasonable use)
- Holidays: 12 paid holidays
- Bereavement: Up to 5 days paid

Other Benefits:
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Commuter benefits (pre-tax)
- FSA/HSA accounts
- Free Chinook Premium subscription
- $100/month music allowance
- Monthly wellness stipend ($75)
- Annual learning budget ($2,000)

Paid Time Off (PTO):

Accrual Rates:
- Years 0-2: 15 days/year (accrues 1.25 days/month)
- Years 3-5: 20 days/year (accrues 1.67 days/month)
- Years 6+: 25 days/year (accrues 2.08 days/month)

Carryover:
- Maximum carryover: 10 days
- Must use excess by March 31 of following year
- No payout for unused PTO except at termination

Requesting Time Off:
- Submit requests via HR system (Workday)
- 2+ weeks notice for planned time off
- Manager approval required
- Blackout periods may apply (end of quarter, etc.)

Holidays (2024):

SECTION 4: LEAVE POLICIES
================================================================================

Parental Leave:

Birth Parents:
- 16 weeks fully paid leave
- Additional 4 weeks at 60% pay if needed
- Can be taken in blocks within 12 months of birth
- Begins upon birth or immediately before (if needed)

Non-Birth Parents:
- 12 weeks fully paid leave
- Must be taken within 12 months of birth/adoption
- Can be taken in blocks

Adoption/Foster:
- 12 weeks fully paid leave
- Begins upon placement
- Applies to children under 18

Gradual Return:
- Option to work 60% time for 4 weeks after leave
- Full pay during gradual return period
- Manager coordination required

Medical Leave:

Short-Term:
- First 5 days: Paid sick leave
- Days 6-84: Short-term disability (60% pay)
- Doctor's note required after 3 consecutive days

Long-Term:
- After 12 weeks: Long-term disability kicks in
- 60% of salary, up to $10,000/month
- Benefits continue during approved leave

FMLA:
- Eligible after 12 months employment
- Up to 12 weeks unpaid, job-protected leave
- For serious health condition, family care, or military family leave
- Runs concurrently with paid leave when applicable

Bereavement Leave:

Immediate Family:
- Up to 5 paid days
- Immediate family: Spouse, parent, child, sibling, grandparent
- Additional unpaid time available

Extended Family/Friends:
- Up to 3 paid days
- Extended family: In-laws, aunts/uncles, cousins, close friends
- Manager discretion for additional time

Jury Duty:
- Paid time off for jury service
- No limit on duration
- Provide summons to HR
- Work not expected during service

Voting Leave:
- Up to 2 hours paid to vote in elections
- Coordinate timing with manager
- Applies to all government elections

Military Leave:
- As required by USERRA
- Job-protected leave for service
- Differential pay for up to 12 months
- Benefits continuation

SECTION 7: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

Performance Philosophy:
We believe in continuous feedback and development. Performance management isn't a once-a-year event—it's an ongoing conversation between managers and team members.

Performance Cycle:

Quarterly Check-Ins:
- Formal 1:1 with manager
- Review goals and progress
- Discuss development
- Document in system

Mid-Year Review (July):
- Formal performance assessment
- Goal recalibration
- Development plan update
- No ratings, narrative feedback only

Annual Review (January):
- Comprehensive performance review
- Rating assigned (see below)
- Compensation review
- Goal setting for new year

Performance Ratings:

1 - Needs Improvement:
- Not meeting expectations
- Performance plan required
- No bonus or merit increase

2 - Developing:
- Meeting some expectations
- Improvement needed in key areas
- Below-target bonus, minimal increase

3 - Meeting Expectations:
- Solid, consistent performance
- Delivering on goals
- Target bonus and merit increase

4 - Exceeding Expectations:
- Strong performance
- Going above and beyond
- Above-target bonus and increase

5 - Exceptional:
- Outstanding performance
- Significant impact
- Maximum bonus and increase
- Promotion consideration

Distribution Guidelines:
Ratings are calibrated across the company to ensure fairness:
- Rating 5: ~10% of employees
- Rating 4: ~25% of employees
- Rating 3: ~50% of employees
- Rating 2: ~10% of employees
- Rating 1: ~5% of employees

Goal Setting:

OKR Framework:
We use Objectives and Key Results (OKRs):
- Objectives: Qualitative goals (what you want to achieve)
- Key Results: Measurable outcomes (how you'll know you achieved it)

Guidelines:
- 3-5 objectives per quarter
- 2-4 key results per objective
- 70% achievement is success (stretch goals)
- Cascade from company to team to individual

Performance Improvement:

Performance Improvement Plan (PIP):
For employees with Rating 1 or persistent issues:
- Duration: 30-90 days
- Specific, measurable goals
- Weekly manager check-ins
- Clear consequences if not improved
- HR involvement required

Outcomes:
- Successful: Return to normal performance management
- Extended: Additional 30 days if progress shown
- Unsuccessful: Employment may be terminated

SECTION 9: HEALTH AND SAFETY
================================================================================

Workplace Safety:

Commitment:
Chinook is committed to providing a safe and healthy workplace for all employees, contractors, and visitors.

General Safety Rules:
- Report hazards immediately
- Keep work areas clean and organized
- Know emergency exits and procedures
- Don't block exits or safety equipment
- Report all injuries, no matter how minor

Emergency Procedures:

Fire:
1. Activate nearest alarm
2. Evacuate via nearest exit
3. Meet at designated assembly point
4. Don't use elevators
5. Don't re-enter until all-clear given

Medical Emergency:
1. Call 911
2. Contact reception/security
3. Don't move injured person unless necessary
4. Provide first aid if trained
5. Stay with person until help arrives

Earthquake (California offices):
1. Drop, cover, hold on
2. Stay away from windows
3. After shaking stops, evacuate if building damaged
4. Check for injuries
5. Be prepared for aftershocks

Active Shooter:
1. Run if you can escape
2. Hide if you can't escape
3. Fight as last resort
4. Call 911 when safe
5. Follow law enforcement instructions

Ergonomics:
- Workstation assessment available
- Ergonomic equipment provided on request
- Sit-stand desks available
- Regular break reminders encouraged

Mental Health:

Support Resources:
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Free counseling
- Mental health coverage in insurance
- Wellness app subscription provided
- Mental health days allowed (use sick leave)

Reducing Stress:
- Manageable workloads expected
- Take your PTO
- Disconnect after hours
- Speak up if overwhelmed
- Manager check-ins on wellbeing

COVID-19 and Illness Policies:

Current Guidelines:
- Vaccination encouraged but not required
- Stay home if sick
- Masks optional (may change based on conditions)
- Sanitization supplies provided
- Improved ventilation in offices

Returning from Illness:
- No symptoms for 24 hours
- COVID-19: Follow current CDC guidelines
- Doctor's note for extended absence

SECTION 10: TERMINATION AND OFFBOARDING
================================================================================

Types of Termination:

Voluntary Resignation:
- Employee-initiated departure
- 2-week notice expected (4 weeks for managers+)
- Exit interview conducted
- Final paycheck per state law

Involuntary Termination:
- Company-initiated separation
- May be for cause or without cause
- Severance per policy (below)
- COBRA information provided

Layoff/Reduction in Force:
- Due to business conditions
- Selection based on business needs
- Severance provided
- Outplacement support available

Resignation Process:

Steps:
1. Notify manager verbally first
2. Submit written resignation to HR
3. Work notice period (if applicable)
4. Complete offboarding checklist
5. Return company property
6. Exit interview with HR

Notice Period:
- Non-management: 2 weeks
- Manager: 3 weeks
- Director+: 4 weeks
- May be paid in lieu of working

Offboarding Checklist:
- Return laptop and equipment
- Return badge and keys
- Transfer knowledge to colleagues
- Update documentation
- Complete exit interview
- Remove personal items

Severance Policy:

Eligibility:
- Involuntary termination without cause
- Layoff/reduction in force
- Not for termination with cause

Standard Severance:
- 2 weeks pay for each year of service
- Minimum: 4 weeks
- Maximum: 26 weeks
- Health insurance continuation during severance period

Severance Agreement:
- Release of claims required
- 21 days to consider (40+ years old: 21-45 days)
- 7-day revocation period
- Non-disparagement clause

Final Pay:

Timing (California):
- Resignation with notice: Final day
- Resignation without notice: Within 72 hours
- Involuntary termination: Final day

Included in Final Pay:
- Earned wages through last day
- Accrued, unused PTO
- Any owed expense reimbursements
- Vested bonus amounts

References:
- Chinook provides dates of employment and job title only
- Personal references at individual discretion
- Must use personal contact information for personal references
