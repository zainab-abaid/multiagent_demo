Music Genres at Chinook Store

The Chinook Music Store offers tracks across 25 different genres, catering to diverse musical tastes.

Popular Genres Include:
- Rock: The most popular genre with hundreds of tracks
- Latin: Features music from Latin American artists
- Metal: Heavy metal and hard rock tracks
- Alternative: Alternative rock and indie music
- Jazz: Classic and modern jazz recordings
- Blues: Traditional and contemporary blues
- Classical: Orchestral and chamber music
- Country: Country and western music
- Pop: Popular music and top 40 hits
- R&B/Soul: Rhythm and blues and soul music

Genre Statistics:
- The store maintains a balanced catalog across all genres
- Rock and Pop genres typically have the highest sales volumes
- Latin music has seen significant growth in recent years, especially in 2013
- Classical and Jazz genres have a dedicated customer base

World:

Hip Hop/Rap:
