SECTION 3: METADATA MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

Required Metadata Fields:

Track Level:
- Track title (required)
- Track artist (required)
- ISRC (required or auto-generated)
- Duration (required)
- Track number (required)
- Genre (required)
- Explicit flag (required)
- Composers/writers (recommended)
- Release date (required)

Album Level:
- Album title (required)
- Album artist (required)
- UPC/EAN (required or auto-generated)
- Release date (required)
- Record label (required)
- Album type (album, EP, single, compilation)
- Genre (required)

Quality Scoring:
Each release receives a metadata quality score (0-100):
- Required fields complete: 40 points
- Recommended fields complete: 20 points
- Formatting standards met: 15 points
- No errors: 15 points
- Rich metadata: 10 points

Minimum score for publication: 70
Target average score: 90+

SECTION 7: QUALITY ASSURANCE
================================================================================

Quality Standards:

- No distortion or clipping

- Clean silence at boundaries

- All required fields complete
- Consistent formatting
- Accurate attribution

Artwork:
- Minimum: 3000×3000 pixels
- Square aspect ratio
- No misleading imagery
