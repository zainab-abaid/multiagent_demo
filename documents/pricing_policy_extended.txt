Chinook Music Store - Complete Pricing, Currency, and Payment Policy Guide

================================================================================
SECTION 1: PRICING PHILOSOPHY AND OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Pricing Mission:
Chinook Music Store is committed to fair, transparent, and accessible pricing that balances value for customers with fair compensation for artists and rights holders.

Core Pricing Principles:
1. Transparency: All prices clearly displayed with no hidden fees
2. Fairness: Consistent pricing across similar products
3. Artist Support: Pricing that enables sustainable artist royalties
4. Global Access: Pricing adapted for different markets while maintaining fairness
5. Quality Value: Premium pricing only for genuine quality improvements

Pricing Governance:
- Pricing Committee meets monthly to review and adjust prices
- Annual comprehensive pricing review in January
- Emergency pricing reviews as needed for market conditions
- All pricing changes announced 30 days in advance

SECTION 2: TRACK PRICING STRUCTURE
================================================================================

Average Track Price:
The store's average price per track across all categories is $1.05 USD. This average is calculated based on the weighted distribution of tracks across all pricing categories in the catalog.

Standard Track Pricing:

Base Price Categories:

Category A - Standard Catalog ($0.99 USD)
- Back catalog tracks (3+ years old)
- Independent artist releases
- Standard genre content
- Re-releases and compilations
- This represents approximately 75% of the catalog

Category B - Current Releases ($1.29 USD)
- New releases (within first 90 days)
- Chart-performing tracks
- Featured playlist content
- Major artist new content
- This represents approximately 15% of the catalog

Category C - Premium Content ($1.49 USD)
- Exclusive releases
- Limited edition content
- Live recordings
- Rare and archival material
- This represents approximately 7% of the catalog

Category D - Ultra-Premium ($1.99 USD)
- Masters quality tracks
- Exclusive premieres
- Special edition content
- Collector's items
- This represents approximately 3% of the catalog

Average Price Calculation:
Based on the catalog distribution across these categories, the weighted average price per track is $1.05 USD. This average accounts for the proportion of tracks in each pricing category and is used for estimating total values when calculating potential revenue or costs for large track collections.

Price Transition Schedule:
- New releases start at Category B or C
- After 90 days: Evaluation for price reduction
- After 180 days: Most tracks move to Category A
- Evergreen content may remain at higher tiers longer

SECTION 4: AUDIO QUALITY PRICING
================================================================================

Format and Quality Tiers:

Standard Quality (Included in base price):
- MP3 256 kbps
- File size: ~2MB per minute
- Suitable for: Casual listening, mobile devices
- No additional charge

High Quality (Included in base price):
- MP3 320 kbps
- AAC 256 kbps
- File size: ~2.5MB per minute
- Suitable for: Most listening scenarios
- No additional charge

Lossless Quality (Premium):
- FLAC 16-bit/44.1kHz (CD quality)
- ALAC 16-bit/44.1kHz
- File size: ~10MB per minute
- Additional cost: +$0.50 per track
- Additional cost: +$3.00 per album

Hi-Res Audio (Premium):
- FLAC 24-bit/96kHz
- FLAC 24-bit/192kHz
- File size: ~25MB per minute (96kHz), ~50MB (192kHz)
- Additional cost: +$1.00 per track
- Additional cost: +$6.00 per album
- Availability: Limited to participating labels

Masters Quality (Ultra-Premium):
- Original master quality
- MQA encoded files
- Studio approval required
- Additional cost: +$1.50 per track
- Additional cost: +$10.00 per album
- Availability: Select catalog only (~5%)

Quality Upgrade Pricing:
Customers who previously purchased lower quality can upgrade:
- MP3 to Lossless: Pay difference ($0.50/track or $3.00/album)
- MP3 to Hi-Res: Pay difference ($1.00/track or $6.00/album)
- Lossless to Hi-Res: Pay difference ($0.50/track or $3.00/album)
- No upgrade fee for purchases within 30 days

SECTION 8: REFUND AND RETURN POLICY
================================================================================

General Policy:
Digital music downloads are generally non-refundable once delivered, as the product cannot be "returned" in the traditional sense. However, Chinook provides refunds in the following circumstances:

Eligible Refund Situations:

Technical Issues:
- Download failure (file corruption)
- Playback issues (defective file)
- Wrong file delivered
- Missing tracks in album purchase
Resolution: Full refund or replacement file

Duplicate Purchases:
- Accidental double-purchase of same track/album
- Previous purchase within 30 days
Resolution: Full refund of duplicate purchase

Billing Errors:
- Charged incorrect amount
- Unauthorized charges
- Currency conversion errors
Resolution: Refund of overcharge or full refund

Quality Issues:
- Audio quality significantly below advertised
- Incomplete or cut-off recordings
- Mislabeled content (wrong song/artist)
Resolution: Full refund or replacement

Pre-Order Cancellations:
- Cancellation before release date
- Release date delayed by more than 30 days
Resolution: Full refund

Non-Refundable Situations:
- Change of mind after download
- Dislike of music after purchase
- Device compatibility issues (if format was clearly stated)
- Downloaded and listened to complete album/track
- Purchase made more than 90 days ago

Refund Process:

Requesting a Refund:
1. Log into your Chinook account
2. Go to Purchase History
3. Select the item for refund
4. Click "Request Refund"
5. Select reason from dropdown
6. Provide additional details if needed
7. Submit request

Processing Times:
- Review: 1-2 business days
- Decision notification: Email within 3 business days
- Refund processing: 5-7 business days after approval
- Credit card refunds: 3-5 additional business days to appear

Refund Methods:
- Original payment method (preferred)
- Chinook account credit (immediate)
- PayPal refund (if originally paid via PayPal)

Refund Limits:
- Maximum refunds per customer per year: 10
- Excessive refund requests may result in account review
- Suspicious refund patterns may result in account suspension

SECTION 10: TAX AND FEES
================================================================================

Sales Tax:
Chinook collects and remits sales tax where required by law. Tax rates vary by jurisdiction and are calculated at checkout.

United States:
- Tax calculated based on customer billing address
- Rates vary by state, county, and city
- Tax-exempt purchases: Provide valid exemption certificate

European Union:
- VAT included in displayed prices for EU customers
- Standard VAT rates apply by country
- VAT rate range: 17% (Luxembourg) to 27% (Hungary)
- B2B: VAT reverse charge available with valid VAT ID

United Kingdom:
- VAT (20%) included in displayed prices

Canada:
- GST/HST calculated based on province
- Rates: 5% (Alberta) to 15% (Nova Scotia, etc.)

Australia:
- GST (10%) included in displayed prices

Japan:
- Consumption Tax (10%) included in displayed prices

Other Jurisdictions:
- Local taxes calculated where applicable
